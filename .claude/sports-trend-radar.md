- Do not publish or interact with X.
- Follow the source hierarchy and verification rules defined in `docs/x-growth/RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md` for every sport researched. Attempt Tier 1 (official) sources first; only descend to lower tiers if required information is unavailable there; never let a lower tier override an official source.
